# Sound credits

Every file in this folder is released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0): no
attribution is required, and they may be used in any project. The credits
below are given anyway, with the page each file came from.

Files were re-encoded for the web: music as 128 kbps MP3, the short sounds as
MP3 (96 kbps). The engine loop is the original WAV.

| File | What it is | Author | Licence | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `music-day.mp3` | "Exploration Theme", the day theme | Cleyton Kauffman | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/exploration-theme |
| `music-night.mp3` | "Lost in the Snow Wave", the night theme (loopable synthwave) | hatmix | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/node/172854 |
| `sfx/ui-click.mp3`, `sfx/ui-switch.mp3`, `sfx/ui-open.mp3`, `sfx/ui-confirm.mp3`, `sfx/ui-close.mp3`, `sfx/ui-drop.mp3` | Interface clicks, switch, open, confirm, close and a soft drop (from the "Interface Sounds" pack) | Kenney (kenney.nl) | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds |
| `sfx/car-engine.wav` | Racing car engine loop | Not named on the page | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops |
| `sfx/heli-rotor.mp3` | Helicopter rotor loop, the engine of the helicopter | aquinn (the name linked on the page; not confirmed as the author) | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/helicopter-sounds |
| `sfx/alarm.mp3` | A siren, its first second or so is the warning when a timed mission is nearly out of time | aquinn (the name linked on the page; not confirmed as the author) | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/sirens-and-alarm-noise |

The sea, rain, wind, thunder, birdsong, crickets, the plane's drone, and the
whoosh and splash effects are generated in the browser (`src/core/audio.js`)
and need no files. So are the car radio's four stations (`src/core/radio.js`) and the short game sounds: footsteps, jumps, landings, the horn and the mission jingles (`src/core/sfx.js`).
