Flying the plane
  1. Hold R (or Throttle +) to add power. The plane lifts off at about 80 km/h.
  2. Once it's fast enough, push W to lift the nose.
  3. A and D bank and turn. S points the nose down.
  4. To land, ease off with F, level the nose, and line up with the strip.
